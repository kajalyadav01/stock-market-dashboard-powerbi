# Global Stock Market Performance Dashboard | 2015–2024

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 10 years of stock market data 
across 10 global companies including Apple, Nvidia, Microsoft, Amazon and Google.

## Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard](Screenshot%2026-05-11%235557.png)

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query (data cleaning)
- DAX (calculated measures)
- Dataset: Kaggle — Top 10 Global Companies Stock Data

## Key Insights
- NVDA price grew 840% from 2020 to 2024, driven by AI chip demand surge
- AAPL maintained 2nd highest daily trading volume at 142M shares/day over 10 years
- 2022 marked major downturn — MSFT, GOOG and META dropped 30–40% due to global rate hikes
